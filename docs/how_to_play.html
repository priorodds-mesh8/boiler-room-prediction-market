Practice Run Guide

Forecasting as human-plus-AI signal consolidation.

Boiler Room turns enterprise sales uncertainty into a live market where ML, AI agents, and human sales judgment combine into one consensus probability. The game is not about making the biggest trade. It is about discovering who adds signal and who adds noise.

What you are doing

Play the probability desk.

Each run gives you a set of live sales-deal questions. For every deal, you set an honest probability and decide whether to buy, sell, or hold. Your probability is scored separately, while your trade can move the consensus market.

Pick a run shape.

Choose Team, Agent Focus, or Silent Market; then select deal count and run length. Longer runs give signals more time to accumulate.

Read the ML prior and consensus market.

The ML prior is the machine-only starting point. Consensus is the live price after market activity.

Inspect the priority stack.

Cards surface metric, process, agent, and rumor signals. Rumors are intentionally noisy; process and role-specific cards should matter more.

Submit your forecast book.

Set probability, choose BUY, SELL, or HOLD, and size the stake. Stake affects market movement and P&L. Probability affects calibration score.

Settle and learn.

At the end, hidden outcomes resolve. The debrief compares ML prior, consensus market, and your forecast with Brier score.

The product theory

The market is the intelligence layer.

Older corporate prediction markets were often human-only aggregation systems. Boiler Room's bet is that the stronger enterprise workflow combines broad ML priors, tireless AI-agent decomposition, and human social intelligence inside the same market.

ML Prior

Machine pattern recognition

The model sees broad trends across historical deal data, stages, dates, amounts, process status, and similar outcomes.

AI Agent Read

Structured decomposition

Agents read the deal through sales, legal, security, procurement, and technical lenses. They can be scored and tuned like contributors.

Human Read

Social context

Reps and VPs know champion politics, buyer mood, sandbagging, urgency, and executive pressure that raw CRM often misses.

The important claim

The goal is not to prove that markets always beat ML. The goal is to measure whether human-plus-AI consensus increases information signal over isolated machine learning, isolated agents, or isolated human judgment.

signal lift = Brier(earlier signal layer) - Brier(later signal layer)
How winning works

Lower Brier wins.

Brier score is squared forecast error. It rewards calibrated probabilities, not bravado. A 70% forecast on a true outcome scores 0.090. A 30% forecast on that same true outcome scores 0.490.

Score What it answers Why it matters
Brier Was the probability calibrated? Primary game score and best single measure for honest forecasting.
Log loss Did overconfidence get punished? Useful for evaluating whether a layer is dangerously certain.
Directional accuracy Was the forecast on the right side of 50%? Easy to explain, but less important than calibration.
Signal lift Did a new layer improve on the prior layer? Core proof metric for human-plus-AI consensus.
How we test it

Run 500 scenarios.

The evaluator runs deterministic games across modes, deal counts, run lengths, and FNG policies. It checks mechanical health and measures ML prior, AI-agent market, final consensus market, and FNG performance.

Command

npm run evaluate:gameplay

Generates summary JSON, Markdown, run-level CSV, and deal-level CSV under data/generated/evaluation/.

Acceptance

On track means measurable signal.

500/500 scenarios should settle, scoring should be internally consistent, and consensus should be evaluated against both ML prior and AI-agent market.

Talk track

The short version.

Boiler Room is a revenue-forecasting game and evaluation loop for measuring information signal. ML supplies the prior, AI agents decompose the deal, humans add field intelligence, and the consensus market compresses the combined judgment into a live probability. Settlement tells us whether that combination reduced entropy or added noise.
